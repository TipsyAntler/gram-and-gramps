Their life

A timeline of the world behind the stories.

This is intentionally a family timeline, not a complete genealogy: the fixed points that help place the voices, photographs, jobs, places, and memories in time.

1925

Sam is born in Hoboken.

The family tree and later records place his early life in Hoboken, where many of the childhood stories begin.

1927

Marietta is born in Hoboken.

Her stories return again and again to Hoboken streets, family, school, work, and the dental office.

1943–46

Sam serves during World War II.

His records and obituary identify U.S. Navy Armed Guard service aboard merchant vessels.

1945

V-J Day in Vancouver.

The setting behind the missing Navy hat story and one of the family's most enduring wartime memories.

1947

Sam and Marietta marry.

The courtship story reaches the point it was always heading toward.

1948

Conrad is born.

A milestone remembered in Story 024, with its own wonderfully specific family details.

1950s

Dental-office years.

Many of Gram's wildest Hoboken stories cluster around Dr. Peluso's office.

1962

Stevens years begin.

Marietta's long stretch at Stevens Institute becomes another rich source of stories.

1976

The twins arrive.

A new generation enters the photographs—and eventually the 2018 recording itself.

1977

Sam becomes Hoboken Postmaster.

A culmination of his long Post Office career.

1986

Retirement.

Both Sam and Marietta close major chapters of their working lives.

2018

The oral history is recorded.

One family afternoon becomes the source recording for this entire project.

2022

75 years of marriage.

A final extraordinary milestone in a life built together.

Still growing

Every photo and record sharpens the picture.

The timeline will keep getting richer as the family identifies photographs, the museum material is digitized, and historical records confirm dates and places.
